ⓘ How We Count Registrations

1. Participants: Count of unique people identified by phone number, normalized name, and age rules.

2. Submissions: Total individual form rows submitted across all three Google response sheets.

3. Competition Entries: Sum of every single competition or category selected across all participants.

4. Combined Total: Plain sum of unique participants from General + Crown + Wonder Women.

5. ≈ Unique Individuals: Cross-form deduped individuals across forms sharing the same phone and normalized name.
